# CasualSites — Anti-Generic Checklist

Reject or redesign the output if:

- it looks like a standard SaaS landing page
- every section has the same width, padding, and reveal timing
- the hero is just headline + subcopy + two buttons + mockup
- there is no major transformation section
- the animation is mostly fade-up-on-scroll
- the color system feels random
- the typography is too small or timid
- the page could fit any startup without changing the copy
- the layout relies on repeated cards instead of directed composition
- the page feels assembled from components instead of designed as an experience
- the CTA appears abruptly without narrative buildup
- the mobile version is just a broken copy of desktop choreography
- the explanation is longer than the actual design decisions
- the agent talks too much and ships too little

Pass only when the site feels authored, specific, smooth, and memorable.
