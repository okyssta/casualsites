# CasualSites — Premium Animation Patterns

## 1. Path growth
A line, beam, route, branch, waveform, or timeline grows as the user scrolls.
Use for brands that want progress, expansion, impact, journey, or evolution.

## 2. Pinned chapter reveal
A section stays pinned while its internal layers reveal in sequence.
Use for storytelling, campaign pages, annual reports, launches, and premium landing pages.

## 3. Expanding crop
Start with an aggressively cropped image or object. As the user scrolls, the crop opens and the full world is revealed.
Use for dramatic visual payoff.

## 4. Typography takeover
A small message scales into a dominant headline, then fragments or transitions into the next section.
Use when the message itself is the emotional anchor.

## 5. Density shift
The page begins sparse and quiet, then grows denser and more energized as more layers, data points, or visuals arrive.
Use for growth and momentum narratives.

## 6. Stack to spread
Cards, panels, or screenshots begin stacked and compressed, then spread into a wider ecosystem.
Use for product systems and community/world-building stories.

## 7. Object-led chaptering
A central visual object remains present through several sections while its scale, color, crop, and context change.
Use for premium identity-led experiences.

## 8. Percentage or milestone advance
A visible number, meter, state label, or stage marker advances during scroll.
Use for journey, roadmap, transformation, or progress storytelling.

## 9. Controlled contrast cut
After several rich sections, transition to a much cleaner scene with fewer elements and a stronger CTA.
Use to make the ending feel intentional and earned.
